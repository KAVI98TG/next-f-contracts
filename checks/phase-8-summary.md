# Phase 8 Summary

Release: V0.9.0

Phase 8 establishes the reusable Forms and Leads layer for every future NEXT F customer website.

The layer deliberately separates:

- mutable Form definitions from immutable Submission snapshots
- client UX validation from authoritative server acceptance
- public Form rendering from private Submission/Lead records
- consent capture from legal conclusions
- spam signals from spam decisions
- Form Submissions from business Leads
- Lead source from future Marketing attribution
- operational lead timeline from the future global Event Registry
- conversion mapping from vendor-specific advertising code
